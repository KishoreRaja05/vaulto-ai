# 50/30/20 Rule

The 50/30/20 rule is a simple way to split your monthly salary into needs (50%), wants (30%) and savings (20%).

## Needs

Needs are spending required for housing, food, health, legal compliance, work and family responsibilities. These are the expenses that must be funded before the other buckets are considered.

## Wants

Wants are spending that adds comfort, status, entertainment or speed, but whose absence does no structural harm. They fit inside the 30% bucket after needs are covered.

## Savings

Savings include SIPs, stocks, RD/FD additions, PPF, NPS, VPF, emergency fund top-ups and extra loan principal. Minimum EMI and insurance premiums do not count because they are needs.

## How it compares with zero-based budgeting

The 50/30/20 rule uses broad three-bucket guardrails and takes less effort than zero-based budgeting. Zero-based budgeting is very detailed, needs higher weekly or monthly review effort and is best for tight budgets, debt payoff and people who overspend. The 50/30/20 rule is best for simple tracking with higher or steady income.

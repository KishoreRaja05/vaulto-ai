# Reducing Transport Spending

How can you reduce what you spend on transport? Total transport cost is more than EMI and petrol, because a vehicle also costs insurance, depreciation, servicing, parking, tolls and challans.

## Total cost of ownership

Total transport cost is EMI plus fuel (petrol/diesel/CNG/charging), insurance, depreciation, servicing and repairs, parking, tolls (FASTag) and challans.

For example, a two-wheeler with ₹3,000 EMI, ₹2,500 fuel, ₹600 service and ₹300 parking costs about ₹6,400 a month, before insurance and depreciation.

## Strategies

- Commute efficiency: metro, bus or local train monthly pass, carpool or shared rides, and work-from-home days.
- Insurance and renewals: compare renewal quotes, protect No Claim Bonus, avoid unneeded add-ons, and renew before expiry.
- Vehicle running cost: keep tyre pressure correct, service regularly, combine errands into one trip, and avoid idling and rush hours.

## High-impact actions

1. Use passes for regular routes: monthly metro, bus or train passes usually beat daily cab or auto fares.
2. Set rules for Ola/Uber/Rapido: use them for late nights, heavy rain, airport trips or groups. For daily commutes, a pass or your own vehicle is cheaper.
3. Trip chaining: combine errands to save fuel.
4. Re-quote insurance each year: compare motor insurance quotes online while keeping adequate cover.
5. Track total commute share: aim to keep transport within roughly 10%-15% of in-hand income.

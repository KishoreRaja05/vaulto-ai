# Recurring Subscriptions

What are recurring subscriptions and why do they matter? Small monthly charges are easy to ignore, but they add up over a year.

## The leakage problem

Annual subscription cost is the sum of monthly rates times 12 for monthly plans, plus any annual fees. For example, Netflix ₹499 + Prime ₹299 + Spotify ₹119 + a ₹1,000 gym fee + cloud storage ₹130 = ₹2,047 a month, about ₹24,500 a year. If you actively use only two of them, the rest is waste.

## The C.A.N. audit

- Cancel: not used in the last 30 days, or little real value.
- Alternate: keep one OTT app active at a time and rotate month to month.
- Negotiate: switch to annual or family/shared plans, downgrade the tier, or use telecom bundles if you truly use them.

## Action steps

1. List everything: check your bank and card statements, UPI AutoPay mandates and app store subscriptions.
2. Cancel unused services immediately.
3. Rotate OTT apps: binge one platform, cancel, then move to the next.
4. Check annual plans: for services you use daily, annual billing often costs less than 12 monthly payments.

## Implementation tips

- Review AutoPay mandates: look at active mandates in your UPI apps, net banking and credit card standing instructions, and revoke ones you no longer need.
- Free trials: use virtual or single-use cards if your bank offers them, and set a reminder 3 days before the trial ends.
- Renewal reminders: add calendar alerts 3 days before annual renewals.
